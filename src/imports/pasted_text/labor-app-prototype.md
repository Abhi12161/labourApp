Aap jo app ka prototype chahte hain, uska main purpose hai ek platform create karna jahan consumers aur laborers (jaise ki raj mistri aur labourers) ek doosre se connect ho sakte hain. Main aapko basic prototype ka description deta hoon, jisme dono login options hain aur categories aur requirements define karne ka option bhi hai. Yeh system aapke described requirements ke mutabiq hoga.

Concept Overview:
Login System:
Consumer Login: Consumer jo labour ya raj mistri ki requirements rakhte hain, wo login karenge.
Labour Login: Labour aur raj mistri jo kaam dene ke liye available hain, wo apna profile bana kar login karenge.
Dashboard Features:
Jab Labour login karega, to usse categories ki list dikhegi, jisme woh raj mistri, labourer, ya kisi specific category ka kaam dekh sakta hai.
Jab consumer login karega, to usse yeh option milega ki wo requirement submit kare:
Kitne raj mistri chahiye.
Kitne labourers chahiye.
Kaunse skills chahiye.
Location (jahan work chahiye, jaise Muzaffarpur).
Budget/Price jo consumer pay karna chahta hai.
Labourers and Raj Mistri Profiles:
Location-based search: Jaise Muzaffarpur, consumer apne location ke hisaab se raj mistri aur labourers search kar sakega.
Profile Details: Labour aur raj mistri apna profile complete karenge jisme unka name, experience, skills, aur location hoga.
Requirements Submission (for Consumers):
Consumer apne requirement form ko fill karega, jisme wo specify karega:
Kitne labourers aur raj mistri chahiye.
Kaunse skills chahiye (e.g., carpentry, plumbing, construction work).
Price range.
Location where the work is needed.
Deadline for the work.
Laborers Matching with Requirements:
Jab consumer apna requirement submit karega, app automatically match karega available labourers and raj mistri with consumer’s requirement and location.
Sign-Up & Profile Setup:
Consumers will fill in details like name, email, location (Muzaffarpur or any location), and contact information.
Labourers/Raj Mistri will fill in their details like name, skills, years of experience, location (Muzaffarpur or surrounding areas), and contact details.
Matching System:
Once the consumer fills the requirement form, the system will look for labourers/raj mistri based on:
Location
Skills required
Price range
Availability
Location Filtering:
Consumers can filter the results based on location (like Muzaffarpur) to find the nearest available workers.
Prototype Flow (Simplified)
Landing Page:
Login options for Consumer and Labour/Raj Mistri.
Option to Sign Up if the user is new.
Consumer Login:
After login, consumer sees a dashboard with an option to submit a new requirement.
Requirement Form:
Location: Dropdown for cities (Muzaffarpur, Patna, etc.)
Skills required: Select from options like labour, raj mistri, carpenter, plumber, etc.
Number of workers needed: Enter number of labourers and raj mistri.
Budget/Price: Enter the price range they are willing to pay.
Submit Request button.
Labour/Raj Mistri Login:
After login, labourers see a list of available jobs or requirements submitted by consumers.
They can apply for the job, see details like location, skills needed, and price.
Option to update profile with location, skills, and availability.
Location-based Matching:
When a consumer submits their requirement, the system matches it with labourers/raj mistri in the same location.
Example: A consumer from Muzaffarpur wants 4 labourers for a construction project. The app will show available labourers and raj mistri from Muzaffarpur or nearby areas.
Notifications:
Both consumers and workers will get notifications for new job matches, applications, and updates.
Feedback/Rating:
After a task is completed, consumers can rate the labourer or raj mistri based on performance, and vice versa.
Key Features:
User-friendly Interface: Simple and intuitive design for both consumers and labourers.
Real-time Matching: Consumers’ requirements are matched instantly with available workers based on skills and location.
Location-based Search: Filter by location (e.g., Muzaffarpur).
Secure Payments: Option to handle payments through the platform (optional feature).
Feedback System: For consumer and worker ratings after work is completed.
